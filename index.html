01 / ABOUT

A builder from Thrissur.

I'm Ajmal — a software developer who got hooked on computers the way most people get hooked on a good book. Somewhere between debugging my first C program and watching a motor spin because of code I wrote, I realized this was it.

These days I spend my time building agentic AI systems, hacking on IoT & embedded hardware, and bringing Atom Innovations and Atom Skill Academy to life — both on the way with solid roadmaps and real traction. The best way to learn is to teach, and the second best way is to build the thing you wish existed.

I volunteer with IEEE and intern as an IoT Assistant for the PM Vikas programme. I love mentoring students through their first projects, and believe that the best portfolios are the ones you can plug in and use. I'm passionate about making tech accessible and building communities where curious people can learn and build together.
